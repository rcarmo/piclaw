Family memory

Publishing copies your chosen text for every family account. Private source identifiers stay in your receipt. Publisher attribution does not prove authorship or truth. No Dream, file or prompt projection is enabled.

Drafts, inspected text and retry keys clear on refresh, close, focus loss, session change or navigation. A sent request may finish. Inspect history before creating another publication after an uncertain response.

It is not a full backup or an atomic database snapshot. Each message is limited to 32,000 characters; truncation is marked. Maximum: 2,000 messages and 8 MiB; larger exports fail without a partial file.
